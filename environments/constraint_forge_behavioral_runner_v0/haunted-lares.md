([Roman](/wiki/Roman "Roman") [mythology](/wiki/mythology "mythology"), chiefly in the [plural](/wiki/Appendix:Glossary#plural "Appendix:Glossary")) [singular](/wiki/Appendix:Glossary#singular_number "Appendix:Glossary") of *[lares](/wiki/lares#English "lares")*: a [household god](/wiki/household_god "household god"), [particularly](/wiki/particularly "particularly") [overseeing](/wiki/oversee "oversee") the [family](/wiki/family "family") [itself](/wiki/itself "itself").

##### Usage notes

\[[edit](/w/index.php?title=lar&action=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Usage notes")\]

The gibbon is pluralized as *[lars](/wiki/lars#English "lars")*. The Latin household gods usually appear as the *[plurale tantum](/wiki/plurale_tantum#English "plurale tantum")* *[Lares](/wiki/Lares#English "Lares")*, following its Latin plural form and capitalized to denote a particular group of lares; the alternative forms *[Lars](/wiki/Lars#English "Lars")*, *[lares](/wiki/lares#English "lares")*, and *[lars](/wiki/lars#English "lars")* sometimes appear.

## Galician

\[[edit](/w/index.php?title=lar&action=edit&section=30 "Edit section: Galician")\]

[![](//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/fc/Lareira_o_cocina.jpg/250px-Lareira_o_cocina.jpg?utm_source=en.wiktionary.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](/wiki/File:Lareira_o_cocina.jpg)

Traditional *lar* or *lareira*

### Etymology 1

\[[edit](/w/index.php?title=lar&action=edit&section=31 "Edit section: Etymology 1")\]

From [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin "w:Latin") *[larem](#Latin)* (“guardian spirit; home”), from [Etruscan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etruscan_language "w:Etruscan language").

#### Pronunciation

\[[edit](/w/index.php?title=lar&action=edit&section=32 "Edit section: Pronunciation")\]

-   [IPA](/wiki/Wiktionary:International_Phonetic_Alphabet "Wiktionary:International Phonetic Alphabet")([key](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galician_phonology "wikipedia:Galician phonology")): /ˈlaɾ/

#### Noun

\[[edit](/w/index.php?title=lar&action=edit&section=33 "Edit section: Noun")\]

**lar** m (*plural* **[lares](/wiki/lares#Galician "lares")**)

1.  [home](/wiki/home "home") (place or building where one dwells)
    
    **Miña casiña meu **lar**.**
    
    My house, my **home**.
    
2.  [fireside](/wiki/fireside "fireside")
3.  [hearth](/wiki/hearth "hearth")
    -   **1485**, Antonio López Ferreiro, editor, Galicia Histórica. Colección diplomática, Santiago: Tipografía Galaica, page 286:
        
        *iten vnna caldeyra de trager agoa, iten hua caldeyra de sobre do **lar**, iten dous caldeyros de mao*
        
        *item, a bucket for carrying water; item a cauldron for hanging over the **hearth**; item two hand cauldrons*
        
4.  a [household](/wiki/household "household") or ancestral [god](/wiki/god "god") in ancient Rome

\[[edit](/w/index.php?title=lar&action=edit&section=36 "Edit section: Etymology 2")\]
