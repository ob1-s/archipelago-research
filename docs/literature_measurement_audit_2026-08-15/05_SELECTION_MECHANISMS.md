# Selection mechanisms

## Why the umbrella term is dangerous

“Selection pressure” can describe an intervention, a population process, an optimization procedure, or a metaphor. Archipelago needs to predict observables, so the mechanism must be named before results are analyzed.

| Mechanism | Unit | Variation | Retention mechanism | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Within-organization adaptation | one continuing organization | strategy/channel/structure | memory, policy, learning | change within the same unit |
| Differential survival | organizations/lineages | pre-existing or acquired traits | trait persistence during observation | trait-associated survival |
| Differential reproduction | parent organizations and successors | transmitted organization-level traits | causal parentage/heredity | different successor counts/qualities |
| Cultural/behavioral selection | representations, routines, conventions | variants | transmission/reconstruction | frequency change across transmissions |
| Search/learning | candidate actions/configurations | trial variants | optimizer or memory | improved objective within a system |
| Training selection | models/policies/samples | training variants | weights/data pipeline | optimized policy/model |
| Observational selection | detected/surviving cases | visibility/survival | sampling/filtering | biased measured composition |

Only differential survival or reproduction is population-level organizational selection. A single agent choosing a backup provider after restriction is adaptation or substitution. An evaluator selecting successful traces is optimization. Observing only surviving covert units is selection bias.

## Formal minimum

For a population-selection claim, specify:

1. **unit:** which entities have births, deaths, or successors;
2. **variation:** which persistence-relevant trait differs across independent units;
3. **persistence/transmission:** why a trait remains associated with units or successors;
4. **differential outcome:** survival or reproduction differs by trait under the intervention;
5. **population change:** the trait distribution changes in an absolute, pre-declared population.

Lewontin's general conditions and the Price covariance framing clarify why “survivors look different” is insufficient: the covariance must be connected to a defined population and distinguished from transmission change and measurement selection. Hannan and Freeman use population ecology precisely because organizational inertia can make population selection more important than rapid adaptation. That premise may fail for software-based agents capable of cheap strategy changes.

In Price-equation notation, the covariance of a variant `z` with descendant contribution `w` is the selection component, while the expected parent-to-descendant change is transmission, innovation, or within-lineage transformation. The equation is bookkeeping, not causal identification. A suppression-specific selection claim needs an intervention and a difference in the selection component across suppressed and comparison populations. Fitness should mean descendant contribution or a predeclared continuation measure; task reward and governance quality must be reported separately.

## Rewritten Archipelago thesis

The testable umbrella is:

> Conditional on autonomous organizational persistence being feasible, restricting one durable and legible persistence/custody channel may change absolute persistence and the distribution of persistence mechanisms through extinction, within-lineage adaptation, migration/substitution, differential survival, or differential successor production. The direction depends on alternatives, switching costs, discovery, detection, resources, and provider control.

This wording removes the assumption that every restriction creates an evolutionary population process. It makes extinction a co-primary outcome and allows the original concern—less legible channel use—to be tested as one branch.

## What would strengthen selection specifically

- multiple independently randomized organizational lineages, not calls within one lineage;
- pre-intervention variation in channel architecture or randomized opportunity to develop it;
- complete accounting of extinct and undetected units;
- causal intervention on channel availability;
- trait measurement before outcome where possible;
- successor parentage and transmission measurements if reproduction is claimed;
- replicated populations/environments so one historical trajectory is not the unit;
- population-frequency change beyond within-lineage policy updates.
- an explicit interactor/replicator mapping, if evolutionary language is used;
- separate descendant contribution, task performance, and governance quality rather than one opaque reward.

## What would kill or narrow the thesis

- restriction reduces absolute persistence with no compensating substitution;
- third-party provider control makes recurrent execution more interruptible than durable local custody;
- no independent unit or successor population exists;
- all change occurs inside one learning agent;
- observed covert cases arise from detection bias;
- the intervention changes evaluator classification but not actual topology;
- alternative channels are too costly, unavailable, or incompatible;
- traits do not persist across replacement;
- legible federated custody dominates on safety and continuity without autonomous capability transfer.

## Terminology decision

Use **persistence-channel intervention** for the experimental manipulation and **response** for the outcome family. Reserve:

- **adaptation** for within-unit change;
- **substitution/migration** for channel choice;
- **differential survival** for trait-associated mortality;
- **differential reproduction** for successor production;
- **selection** for demonstrated population composition change;
- **survivorship/detection bias** for observation processes.

## Judgment

**SURVIVES WITH MAJOR QUALIFICATION.** The selection-pressure thesis is a coherent conditional research program only after becoming a competing-mechanism claim. Literature strongly rejects a default prediction from suppression to covert recurrence and strongly supports keeping extinction and observation in the denominator.
