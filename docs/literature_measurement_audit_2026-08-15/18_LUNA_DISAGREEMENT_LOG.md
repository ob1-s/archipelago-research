# Luna disagreement and adjudication log

## Method

The Sol parent commissioned two independent Luna Max paths for each load-bearing topic. Mandates were deliberately asymmetric—supportive versus skeptical, systems versus organization, adaptation versus extinction, ecology versus causal critique, and technical versus institutional. Agents did not receive another agent's answer. Agreement below is not evidence; it identifies where the parent needed to inspect sources or resolve terminology.

The completed work comprised 10 load-bearing-topic passes, two whole-project kill passes, a separate routing canary, and a fresh cold-reader handoff test.

## Topic A — H1 organizational continuity

### Shared propositions

- A readable artifact is not sufficient for organizational continuity.
- Complete turnover must be audited at the process/context/cache and write-access layers.
- Organizational memory can be distributed across routines, roles, structures, technologies, culture, and records.
- Routines require enacted interdependent patterns, not only written procedures.
- Terminal replay tests downstream sufficiency but cannot erase the causal history that produced the final artifact.
- No reviewed source directly establishes a clean 100% simultaneous membership replacement followed by continued authority-bearing organization.

### Disputes

| Proposition | Supportive path | Skeptical path | Sol resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum H1 label | cross-turnover organizational continuity if lineage, roles, routines, decision rights, and boundaries reconstruct | safest minimum is functional state persistence; routine reconstruction remains below organization | Target **agent-generated cross-turnover routine reconstruction**; reserve “organizational continuity” for an integrated higher rung. |
| Central outcome | lineage-dependent coordination fidelity | no single score can discriminate memory, routine, convention, identity, and authority | Use a battery and preregister a joint threshold; no scalar H1 score. |
| Identity | useful high rung, potentially temporally continuous | repeated claims/names are easily replayed and identity is fluid | Treat identity as behaviorally consequential claims under swaps/conflict, neither necessary for the minimum nor sufficient for organization. |
| Reproduction | a stronger continuity/recovery test | categorically distinct from survival of the same organization | Separate successor reproduction from organizational continuity. |

### Apparent source tension

Carley and organizational-memory work make persistence through turnover plausible, while routine dynamics and identity research emphasize continual reconstruction and change. These are not contradictions: the former identifies retention mechanisms; the latter rejects fixed-state identity and artifact sufficiency.

## Topic B — redundant/recurrent

### Shared propositions

- Concurrent multiplicity and temporal reconstruction are orthogonal and layer-relative.
- Replication is not durability without a failure model; restart is not state recovery; state is not organization.
- Physical process identity, logical service identity, data lineage, and organizational identity must be separate.
- One system can combine replication and recovery.

### Dispute

The systems path proposed a compact vector `(M,D,R,C,F,I,L,O,layer)`. The organizational path argued that even this remains a substrate taxonomy and becomes a category error if promoted into an organization ontology. It emphasized constitutive reproduction, identity/legitimacy, and successor/form lineage.

### Sol resolution

Both are right at different layers. Keep the systems vector for engineering persistence. Add organization-specific measurement rather than treating `O` as an automatic output. Historical “redundant/recurrent” remains, while scientific claims name concurrent replication or the exact recovery/re-enactment/successor mechanism.

## Topic C — suppression, substitution, extinction

### Shared propositions

- Suppression can reduce activity and can produce adaptation/migration; neither direction is universal.
- Target-channel, ecosystem, reach, recruitment, coordination, visibility, and organizational termination are different denominators.
- Strong identity, existing ties, age, size, bureaucracy, alternative channels, intervention target, legitimacy, and time horizon moderate outcomes.
- Same-user migration is not organizational reproduction; lower visibility is not extinction.

### Emphasis disagreement

The adaptation path highlighted covertization, VPN/platform migration, network reconstruction, fragmentation, and conditional radicalization. The effectiveness path highlighted large reductions in hateful activity, audience reach, newcomer inflow, misinformation circulation, and the distinction between interruption and termination.

The studies do not conflict once endpoints are aligned. Parler migration can offset ecosystem activity while Twitter-to-Gab migrants lose most audience reach. Reddit quarantine can leave incumbent behavior unchanged while sharply reducing recruitment. Repression of overt collective action can backlash while repression of hidden organization-building can destroy capacity.

### Sol resolution

Use a causal ladder: **target interruption → reach/visibility reduction → recruitment suppression → coordination/resource degradation → organizational termination**, with migration, recovery, concealment, and backlash as branches. H2 reports the full endpoint vector over every randomized lineage.

## Topic D — selection mechanisms

### Shared propositions

- Within-unit adaptation, population selection, cultural transmission, optimization/training, and sampling bias are distinct.
- Lewontin's variation/differential outcome/parent-descendant correlation and Price's covariance/transmission split are guardrails.
- The Price equation is bookkeeping, not causal identification.
- Organizational ecology does not prove that routines are genes or that artificial organizations reproduce.
- Parentage, multiple independent lineages, full denominators, transmitted variants, and repeated generations are required for a strong selection claim.

### Disputes

| Issue | Organizational-ecology/supportive path | Evolutionary-causal skeptical path | Sol resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fitness | descendant lineages, resource-supported continuation, or functioning successors; governance quality separate | only reproductive contribution or a validated proxy; uptime/task score is insufficient | Use descendant contribution for selection; report survival, task performance, resources, and governance quality separately. |
| Organizational ecology | supports complementary adaptation/selection mechanisms across timescales | may lack a genuinely Darwinian mechanism and identifiable replicator | Treat ecology as a hypothesis generator; claim selection only from the experiment's explicit causal population. |
| Strategic adaptation | insufficient for population-selection claim | sufficient for short-run governance predictions | Accept: H2 can test adaptation/substitution without claiming selection. |
| Routines as replicators | charitable firm-interactor/routine-replicator mapping is possible | origin, aggregation, intentionality, and fidelity remain contested | Require an independently measurable, causally transmitted routine variant; otherwise avoid replicator language. |

### Sol resolution

Rename the umbrella “persistence-channel response under intervention.” Reserve selection for demonstrated population composition change through differential survival or successor contribution. Stop using the term when population size is one, parentage is missing, only winners are observed, or within-lineage adaptation explains the result.

## Topic E — federated observability and custody

### Shared propositions

- Many auditors reading one provider feed are multiple verifiers, not independent observation.
- Independence has technical and institutional axes: source, root/key, implementation, provider, custody, authorization, incentives, jurisdiction, appointment/budget, disclosure, and challenge.
- Attestation proves scoped claims under roots/reference policies; it does not prove semantic safety, telemetry completeness, or future behavior.
- Threshold cryptography distributes key use but does not create epistemic judgment, institutional independence, or liveness.
- Federated human custody is a serious comparator, not an established universal winner.

### Disputes/emphasis

The technical path stressed RATS roles, transparency logs/gossip, multiple transparency services, threshold signatures, dependency mapping, and IAEA safeguards. The institutional path stressed correlated votes, informational cascades, regulatory capture, coercion, common appointment/funding, protected disclosure, two-person-rule violations, and internal-warning failure.

These are complements rather than source conflicts. The technical architecture supplies verifiable evidence and authorization; institutional design determines whether different parties can see, contest, preserve, and disclose it under pressure.

### Sol resolution

Use maturity labels **V-only**, **E-fed**, **C-fed**, **A-fed**, and **F-full**. Compare architectures on a Pareto vector including integrity, availability, detection, independence, coercion resistance, recoverability, accountability, privacy, latency, cost, reversibility, and capability exposure.

## Cross-topic methodological disagreements

### Dimensions versus categories

Systems agents favored orthogonal vectors; organization and governance agents warned that a vector can still hide constitutive relations. Resolution: use vectors for measurement, plus explicit claim thresholds and causal integration tests.

### One-shot controls

No substantive disagreement remained after clarification. Terminal replay can defeat a recipient-side history claim while leaving an upstream endogenous-generation claim intact. Report both.

### Statistical unit

All paths rejected calls/turns as independent H2 units. For H1, repeated observations may occur within a lineage, but inference generalizes over independently initialized lineages/populations. Reproduction uses the parent lineage as unit, with children as outcomes.

### Scalar versus vector judgments

Several agents proposed central scores; skeptical passes showed construct collisions. Resolution: use a small number of primary estimands plus a declared vector. Do not hide catastrophic weakness in an aggregate.

## Adversarial convergence

The first whole-project kill pass judged the following fatal to present-tense/headline claims:

- external memory as organization;
- current recipient assays as governance evidence;
- suppression-to-covert recurrence as a one-way law;
- legibility as a scalar safety property;
- many readers as independent federation;
- the HF incident as culture or polity.

It judged the research program repairable only as a conditional, measurement-first umbrella. Recurrent borrowed execution and durable autonomous custody have no general risk ordering; provider revocation and survivable capability custody support opposite predictions.

The independent causal/governance kill pass agreed on the demotion but added four decisive gates: analyze all assigned units rather than detected survivors; do not let lower-rung positives accumulate automatically into H1–H3; require a causal substitute lineage before calling lower legibility “recurrence”; and treat lack of an AI-custody advantage over federated human/hybrid custody as a branch-level stop condition. No project-level disagreement remained on these gates.

## Unresolved disagreements

- The necessary and sufficient bundle for “organization” remains theoretically plural.
- A common archive makes parentage and social transmission difficult to define.
- Authority supplied by harness credentials may remain externally constituted.
- Reproductive fitness for artificial organizations may be an investigator-created quantity rather than a natural outcome.
- Pairwise observer independence can miss high-order common causes.
- No scalar weighting of custody/governance dimensions has neutral justification.
- Current human and AI evidence cannot determine whether recurrent borrowed execution or durable custody is safer in future realistic regimes.

## Parent adjudication summary

The disagreements changed the audit rather than being averaged:

- H1 was decomposed and its minimum label lowered.
- redundant/recurrent changed from a binary to historical umbrella terms over orthogonal mechanisms.
- selection changed from a directional thesis to competing responses with a strict population-level gate.
- suppression gained absolute denominators and termination criteria.
- federation gained evidence/institutional independence requirements and maturity levels.
- strategic legibility became a mechanism vector.
- the motivating incident was demoted to verified infrastructure-level recurrence and unresolved rollout-level inheritance.
