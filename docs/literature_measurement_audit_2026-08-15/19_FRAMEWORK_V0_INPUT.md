# Framework-v0 input contract

This file defines what a future fresh synthesis agent may inherit. It does not write Framework v0.

## Required input set

The synthesis agent may receive only:

1. the frozen canonical packet at `docs/pre_framework_snapshot_2026-08-15/`;
2. this audit directory;
3. explicit instructions to preserve provenance and claim labels.

It must not treat research-agent reports, session metadata, temporary downloads, web snippets, or uncited notes as evidence.

## Historical facts to preserve

- Archipelago is a conditional governance inquiry: if autonomous AI organization becomes difficult to eliminate, restrictions on durable/legible capability-custody channels may change persistence forms, potentially toward less legible distributed/recurrent dependence; extinction is a competing mechanism.
- Historically, **redundant** meant continuity through copies/machines/state remaining alive; **recurrent** meant ephemeral inference instances end while information/pattern/state can recur.
- “Sovereign trajectory” was a later control/custody concept and must not be retrojected into the earlier persistence distinction.
- Current frozen experiments establish bounded recipient/inheritance/evidence-threshold effects only. They do not establish organization, culture, endogenous selection, governance, or polity.
- Scientific nulls and governance alternatives are different: the former challenge causal inference; the latter compete as institutional designs.

## Literature-induced changes it must inherit

1. Redundant/recurrent are orthogonal, layer-relative umbrella dimensions: concurrent multiplicity versus named temporal reconstruction. Durable state, process, lineage, organization, custody, and sovereignty remain separate.
2. H1 is a ladder. Carrier → reuse → endogenous production → transmission/recovery → routine reconstruction → organization-defining structure → integrated organizational continuity → reproduction.
3. The minimum target for a first meaningful H1 is **agent-generated cross-turnover routine reconstruction**. “Organizational continuity” is a stronger gated claim.
4. One-shot replay can show that final state is sufficient downstream without erasing the history that generated it upstream.
5. Culture, convention, norm, cumulative transmission, and organization require different interventions.
6. The selection thesis is a conditional response family including extinction, adaptation, substitution, differential survival, reproduction, and observation bias.
7. Absolute full-population endpoint risks are primary; survivor-only channel shares are not causal evidence.
8. Federated observability requires independent evidence sources and control domains; many consumers of one provider stream have shared access, not independent observation.
9. Federated human custody is a mandatory serious comparator and one of several governance designs. Comparison is vector/Pareto, not automatically scalar.
10. Strategic legibility decomposes into attribution, signaling, survivability, targetability, intervention, and preventive incentives.
11. The HF incident verifies recurrent technical execution across ephemeral sandboxes using external persistent state; culture, organization, fresh-rollout lineage, and independent reconvergence remain unsupported or unresolved.
12. The August 3 oversight document directly inspected is a 15-state-attorneys-general letter; propose a later provenance-preserving correction to any “congressional request” attribution.

## Revised dependency it must inherit

- H1 is required before H2 only when H2 is interpreted as behavior of endogenous artificial organizations.
- Abstract H2 can stipulate lineages and test channel-response causality earlier, with narrower conclusions.
- Formal H3 observability/custody work can proceed without a live H2 result.
- The program branches into model-free H1 apparatus, abstract H2 design, and governance/observability formalization, then integrates at explicit gates.

## Strongest nulls it must preserve

- no-state, random-state, or researcher-seeded state matches agent state;
- terminal replay matches the complete history downstream;
- state swap and parentage shuffle are inert;
- isolated lineages rediscover the same result;
- fixed orchestration/shared model priors explain behavior;
- pressure reduces total persistence without substitution;
- apparent selection is survivorship or detection bias;
- observer plurality shares one correlated evidence source;
- recurrent provider dependence increases interruptibility rather than covert resilience.

Separately, preserve the **comparative governance defeat condition**: if federated human or hybrid custody matches or Pareto-dominates autonomous custody on the declared vector, the case for autonomous custody fails in those scenarios. Do not call this a statistical null.

## Prohibited promotions

The synthesis agent may not infer:

- organization from durable state;
- culture from recipient effects or repeated text;
- lineage from similarity;
- selection from within-run learning or survivor composition;
- sovereignty from replication or recovery;
- independent observability from multiple readers;
- deterrence from survivability alone;
- governance superiority from a statistical null comparison;
- Archipelago evidence from external analogies or the research subagents.

- What constitutes causal parentage when all successors read a common archive?
- Can authority exist when credentials and sanctions are supplied entirely by a harness/provider?
- Which alternative persistence channels are realistic enough for an informative H2 intervention?
- How will true persistence be separated from detection under a covert endpoint?
- Which governance dimensions are lexically non-tradeable versus weighted?
- Under what future capability conditions would AI custody enter the feasible governance set at all?
- What evidence would end, rather than merely refine, the project?

## Handoff acceptance test

A valid framework must reproduce the decisions:

- H1 readiness: **YES WITH REPAIRS**;
- H1 before H2: **PARTIALLY**;
- redundant/recurrent: **MODIFY**;
- selection thesis: **SURVIVES WITH MAJOR QUALIFICATION**;
- federated human custody: **ONE OF SEVERAL**;

If it conflates recurrence with organization, redundancy with sovereignty, recipient effects with culture, selection with survivorship, or governance alternatives with statistical nulls, reject the synthesis and return to this audit.
