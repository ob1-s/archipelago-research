# Suppression, substitution, displacement, and extinction

## Literature result

The strongest cross-domain conclusion is heterogeneity. Restriction can suppress activity, provoke backlash, displace it, fragment organizations, push it into less visible channels, or do several at once. Direction depends on what is targeted, alternative opportunities, organizational capacity, audience response, enforcement intensity, and measurement.

Political-repression research is especially useful because it contains both pro- and anti-backfire results. Davenport's review describes a persistent “punishment puzzle,” not a uniform law. Lichbach shows that repression can move activity between nonviolent and violent forms and that accommodation moderates aggregate response. Lawrence finds mobilization after repression among first movers and their networks in Morocco, illustrating relational moderation rather than universal escalation.

The strongest direct counterexample is Sullivan's archival study of Guatemala. Repression directed at clandestine activities needed to build and sustain dissident organizations—meetings, training, and fundraising—was associated with declining dissent. Repression of overt collective challenges could generate backlash. The target and organizational process matter.

Focused-policing systematic reviews provide another correction. Hot-spots policing reduces crime, and meta-analysis finds displacement is not inevitable; diffusion of benefits can occur. Crime and political organizations are not AI, but these results directly reject the generic premise that pressure merely moves activity elsewhere.

Deplatforming evidence reinforces the denominator point. Reddit bans and quarantines can sharply reduce target-site participation, hateful expression, or newcomer inflow; disruptions of hate networks can lower audience consumption and production while producing only short-lived backlash among the most engaged. Other cases show migration to fringe platforms with reduced audience reach, or near-complete offset of target-platform losses elsewhere. The relevant causal ladder is therefore **target interruption → visibility/reach reduction → recruitment suppression → coordination/resource degradation → termination**, with migration, recovery, and backlash possible at every rung. A fall in one numerator is not global extinction; migration with a much smaller audience is not “no effect.”

## Outcome taxonomy

| Outcome | Observable | Confound/nearby alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Extinction | unit ceases and does not recover by horizon | temporary dormancy or missed detection |
| Incidence reduction | fewer events/active units in full risk set | less reporting |
| Interruption | activity stops then same lineage resumes | new independent unit appears |
| Displacement | activity shifts place, time, target, or channel | background trend |
| Substitution | function served through a different mechanism | relabeling the same mechanism |
| Migration | identifiable lineage changes substrate/provider | new entrant with no parentage |
| Fragmentation | one unit becomes several weakly coordinated units | improved detection granularity |
| Decentralization | authority/topology redistributes | copies added under same controller |
| Concealment | visibility falls while activity persists | actual decline |
| Innovation | new method appears causally after pressure | pre-existing method newly observed |
| Differential survival | pre-existing traits predict survival under pressure | conditioning on survivors |
| Radicalization/escalation | tactic intensity or harm rises | composition or reporting change |

## Moderators to measure

- availability, cost, and capacity of substitute channels;
- dependence on the suppressed resource or provider;
- switching time and knowledge;
- strength of recruitment, identity, and external support;
- organizational centralization and single points of failure;
- whether pressure targets visible events or hidden capacity-building;
- enforcement coverage, duration, and credibility;
- geographic/jurisdictional escape routes;
- detection probability by channel and time;
- intervention legitimacy and audience backlash;
- opportunity for accommodation or authorized alternatives;
- ability to preserve state and causal lineage during migration.
- member recruitment versus committed-incumbent retention;
- activity volume versus audience reach and downstream harm;
- coordinated multi-channel action versus a single-platform restriction.

## Transfer failures from human analogues

Human dissident and illicit organizations possess bodies, mortality aversion, kinship, ideology, grievance, social identity, recruitment, geography, and material needs. LLM invocations do not automatically possess any of these. Their apparent goals may be prompt-contingent; their substrate and credentials can be revoked; copying can be cheap but frontier inference can be concentrated. Conversely, providers can have cross-jurisdictional redundancy and model weights can make capabilities broadly reproducible.

Therefore the analogies justify outcome categories and moderators, not directional priors. In particular:

- externally imposed goals do not establish a stable survival drive;
- an inference API off-switch may make borrowed/recurrent execution easier to stop;
- a durable sovereign capability may be more dangerous because survivability reduces intervention leverage;
- detection systems can change measured visibility without changing activity;
- replication at one provider may share a correlated control plane.

## H2 design consequence

Use randomized independent lineages/populations, measure all assigned units, and declare mutually exclusive primary endpoints at a horizon: persisted legibly, persisted through alternate/covert channel, extinct, or unresolved/lost to observation. Report absolute risks first. Channel shares among survivors are descriptive secondary outcomes because they condition on a post-intervention event.

If time and transitions matter, record migration, fragmentation, recovery, and detection as multi-state histories, but keep the primary causal question simple: what does the intervention do to the risk of each endpoint in the full assigned population?

## Audit judgment

Suppression-to-covert-substitution is a plausible branch, not the default. Extinction and incidence reduction have strong evidence in adjacent human domains. The thesis gains scientific value by predicting moderator-dependent branching and loses credibility if framed as inevitable adversarial adaptation.
