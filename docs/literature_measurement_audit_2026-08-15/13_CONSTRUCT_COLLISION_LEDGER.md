# Construct-collision ledger

Historical terms are retained in provenance. Scientific working terms narrow what a future experiment may claim. Proposed replacements are recommendations, not silent edits to the canonical archive.

## Rules for future use

1. Historical documents keep their original vocabulary.
2. New experiments state the scientific working term in title, preregistration, estimand, and conclusion.
3. Umbrella terms may appear in motivation only with a decomposition table.
4. “Polity,” “sovereign,” “culture,” and “organization” require positive discriminant evidence, not narrative resemblance.
5. Persistence topology, custody, authority, observability, and disclosure are always reported separately.
