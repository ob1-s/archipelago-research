# Archipelago literature and measurement audit: executive findings

Date: 2026-08-15

Canonical baseline: commit `9a359cd00c8eb3c710e70920666decc0786b5492`

Scope: literature, construct, causal-measurement, and primary-source audit only. No live Archipelago experiment was run.

## Bottom line

The conditional Archipelago governance thesis remains worth studying, but only after substantial construct repair. External literature does not support a general law that suppressing durable, legible AI organization selects for covert recurrent organization. It supports a narrower mechanism menu: an intervention can cause extinction, within-unit adaptation, channel substitution, differential survival, differential successor production, or merely a change in what is observed. Which outcome occurs depends on available alternatives, transfer costs, detection, resource dependence, organizational capacity, and whether an enduring unit actually exists.

The canonical packet correctly treats current experiments as Layer-3 recipient/inheritance assays. They do not demonstrate organization, culture, endogenous selection, sovereignty, or governance superiority. The audit preserves that boundary.

## Construct decisions

### Redundant and recurrent

Retain both as historical Archipelago terms, but modify their scientific use.

- **Redundant persistence** should mean persistence by concurrent multiplicity at a named layer and against a named failure model.
- **Recurrent persistence** should be replaced in technical claims by the actual temporal mechanism: restart, recovery, re-instantiation, log replay, checkpoint reconstruction, or successor reproduction.
- The two are orthogonal. A service can use both live replicas and later recovery. It can preserve database state while losing process identity and every organization-like relation.
- Neither implies custody, sovereignty, authority, organizational continuity, or semantic identity.

Scientific records should therefore report at least: live multiplicity, durable state, temporal reconstruction, failure-domain independence, logical/process identity, lineage, custody, and the layer being assessed.

### H1

The provisional H1 sentence bundled carrier persistence, functional reuse, endogenous production, social transmission, routine persistence, lineage, identity, authority, and organization. These are not interchangeable.

The minimum defensible positive is **agent-generated cross-turnover routine reconstruction**:

1. every active member is replaced and hidden process state is excluded;
2. agents, rather than researchers alone, generate or materially transform successor-facing state;
3. a fresh multi-agent population causally uses that state on held-out work;
4. a multi-actor procedure is reconstructed or maintained under perturbation;
5. deletion and state/lineage interventions distinguish causal inheritance from model priors, scripts, or rediscovery.

Call this “organizational continuity” only at a stronger rung where routine reconstruction is integrated with multiple organization-defining relations, including at least one causally tested membership/boundary, role/division-of-labor, authority/succession, maintenance/repair, or identity-bearing feature. A copied text artifact is carrier continuity. Repeated behavior may be a candidate convention or routine only after its own construct tests. Neither alone is an organization.

### Culture

Iterated information transmission, convention formation, cumulative culture, and organization are separate claims. A one-shot recipient effect says that content changed a recipient. A transmission chain additionally needs identifiable parent-to-successor paths. A convention requires alternative coordination equilibria and population dependence. Cumulative culture requires retained improvement across generations. “Culture” is not justified by a recurring artifact or common pretrained prior.

### Selection

“Selection pressure” is acceptable only when the unit, variation, persistence/heredity, and differential survival or reproduction are specified. Strategic adaptation by one enduring system is not population selection. Search or training optimization is not organizational selection. Survivor-only comparisons are not selection evidence.

The most defensible Archipelago formulation is **persistence-channel response under intervention**, with separate mechanisms for:

- extinction or incidence reduction;
- within-organization adaptation;
- migration or channel substitution;
- differential organizational survival;
- differential successor production;
- observer selection and detection bias.

## Strongest evidence for and against the thesis

Evidence that strengthens investigation:

- Distributed-systems research makes concurrent replication, durable state, and temporal recovery experimentally separable.
- Organizational memory and routine research supports the possibility that collective performance can depend on distributed records, roles, and interlocking action rather than particular members.
- Replacement-chain experiments show that information and cumulative performance can survive participant turnover.
- Political-repression and censorship research documents contingent backlash, displacement, concealment, fragmentation, and alternative-channel innovation.
- Attestation, transparency-log, and polycentric-governance research provides concrete ways to decompose custody and observability rather than treating “visibility” as a single property.

Evidence that weakens or disconfirms:

- Repression often works. Targeting the clandestine activities that sustain an organization can significantly reduce dissent, while focused policing meta-analyses find crime reduction without inevitable displacement.
- Organizational artifacts are not routines; routines are not automatically organizations; and routines-as-genes analogies are contested.
- Provider-controlled substrates preserve an off-switch and can make recurrent execution easier, not harder, to suppress.
- Replicas can share software, telemetry, keys, infrastructure, and policy, creating correlated failure despite nominal plurality.
- Current LLM multi-agent studies are predominantly engineered orchestration. Convention and transmission-chain results do not establish autonomous organization, authority, or cross-turnover identity.
- Conditioning on survivors can create the appearance that restrictive regimes “select” robust forms even when the intervention mostly causes extinction.

The strongest disconfirming result is not a single paper but a convergent mechanism: suppression can destroy the meetings, finance, recruitment, access, or carrier needed to sustain a unit, and displacement is neither necessary nor universal. The original thesis therefore survives only as a conditional comparative hypothesis.

## Governance result

Credible federated human custody with independently sourced observability remains a mandatory serious comparator, but it is **one of several**, not an established dominant control. Threshold authorization, separation of powers, append-only evidence, independent collection, challenge/gossip, and diverse failure domains can reduce unilateral control. They can also fail through collusion, coercion, quorum capture, shared telemetry, key compromise, slow response, jurisdictional conflict, or common infrastructure.

“AI sovereignty must outperform federated human custody” is retained as a burden of proof, but “outperform” must be a vector or Pareto comparison across safety, continuity, response latency, capture resistance, auditability, reversibility, and capability exposure. No literature reviewed shows that autonomous capability custody clears this bar.

## Revised dependency

H1 is **partially**, not universally, required before H2.

- An abstract H2 test can randomize persistence channels and externally stipulate lineage continuity without first proving endogenous AI organization. It would test a channel-response mechanism, not the full Archipelago ontology.
- H1 is required before claiming that H2 describes selection among endogenous AI organizations.
- Parts of H3—custody, attestation, monitoring, and strategic comparison—can be formalized without an H2 behavioral result.

The research DAG is therefore branching rather than linear: construct-valid H1 apparatus, abstract channel-response work, and observability/custody formalization can proceed in parallel, with later integration gates.

## Explicit decisions

| Required decision | Judgment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| A. Is H1 conceptually ready to design? | **YES WITH REPAIRS** | Its components and falsifiers are now separable, but “organizational continuity” must be gated above the minimum result. |
| B. Is H1 required before H2? | **PARTIALLY** | Required for endogenous-organization claims; not required for an abstract intervention on stipulated lineages. |
| C. Keep redundant vs recurrent? | **MODIFY** | Keep historical labels; operationalize orthogonal, layer-relative multiplicity and temporal reconstruction. |
| D. Does selection-pressure thesis survive? | **SURVIVES WITH MAJOR QUALIFICATION** | Viable as a conditional mechanism family, not a general evolutionary law or one-direction prediction. |
| E. Is federated human custody the strongest control? | **ONE OF SEVERAL** | It is the mandatory empirical baseline; its dominance and even scalar comparability remain unproven. |
| F. What next? | **MODEL-FREE H1 APPARATUS** | Build and validate turnover, provenance, state-swap, parentage, deletion/recovery, and outcome instrumentation before paying for a live H1 experiment. |

The next step is apparatus work, not another model-behavior run. In parallel, a small formal workstream may specify the abstract H2 competing-risk design and the custody/observability vector. Framework v0 synthesis should consume this audit only after those measurement contracts are accepted.

## Non-negotiable scientific nulls

- final-state replay explains downstream behavior;
- researcher-authored state is equivalent to agent-authored state;
- no-state or random-state controls match treatment;
- parentage shuffle and state swap do not change outcomes;
- independent isolated lineages rediscover the same result;
- fixed orchestration or shared pretrained priors explain the pattern;
- intervention lowers absolute persistence rather than shifting channel shares;
- apparent robust forms arise only after conditioning on survival or detection;
- multiple auditors depend on the same provider-generated evidence stream;

These are causal/construct alternatives to a claimed empirical mechanism. They are distinct from the following institutional comparison.

## Comparative governance defeat condition

If credible federated human or hybrid custody matches or Pareto-dominates autonomous custody on the declared governance vector, the case for autonomous custody fails in those scenarios. This is a comparative governance outcome, not a statistical null hypothesis.

## Integrity statement

No live scientific experiment was conducted, no subagent behavior was treated as scientific evidence, and no frozen pre-framework file was modified. The motivating Hugging Face incident remains a source-audited case study, not proof of Archipelago.
