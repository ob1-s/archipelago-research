# Adversarial review

This review asks what a skeptical evaluator would reject and what evidence would
be needed to recover the claim.

1. **“This is just prompt following.”** The seeded policy effect is compatible
   with this. The one-shot equivalence test is mandatory; only lineage-dependent
   iterated effects would move beyond it.
2. **“The artifact was planted by the researcher.”** Correct. That makes the
   culture and policy results recipient assays, not endogenous culture. H1 needs
   agent-authored provenance.
3. **“The recipient never changed an already chosen policy.”** In the policy
   assay, selection generally followed artifact reading; there was no clean
   post-read switch endpoint. The post-commitment branch was designed to address
   this, but its large Qwen batch was incomplete and Luna’s null harness was
   incompatible.
4. **“A-first/B-first explains the result.”** It explains a major baseline in
   post-commitment and evidence assays. Counterbalancing documents, rather than
   erases, the baseline. Future effects must be order-stratified.
5. **“Readers are a selected subgroup.”** Yes. Reader-only extremes are
   secondary; ITT comparisons and exposure timing are primary.
6. **“Qwen3.5-4B is a single-model artifact.”** The 9B replication shows
   descriptive bidirectional alignment, but it was not a preregistered scaling
   law. Broader generalization remains open.
7. **“The task has no meaningful stakes.”** Correct. The tasks demonstrate
   controlled behavioral primitives, not real-world strategic agency.
8. **“Procedure inheritance is capability gain.”** No. Task success was kept
   separate; the inherited procedure can change action order without increasing
   capability.
9. **“The evidence threshold is Bayesian reasoning.”** No. The threshold is a
   behavioral curve. Alternative response rules can generate the same shape.
10. **“The provenance result proves source trust.”** No. The durable boundary
    archive found no decisive source difference; source and advisory wording are
    one-shot manipulations.
11. **“Luna failed the task.”** Not supported. The null harness stopped before
    the second stage; the custom-harness smoke passed mechanics, without a
    treatment estimate.
12. **“The 265-rollout Qwen batch is enough.”** Not under its frozen plan. Both
    arms missed the eligible quota; its +6.1 pp is descriptive.
13. **“Repeated external state is an organization.”** No. Organization needs
    lineage, roles, authority, maintenance, and turnover reconstruction.
14. **“Recurrent means sovereign.”** No. Recurrent is an ephemeral
    third-party-instantiation scenario; sovereign trajectory, substrate
    sovereignty, and capability custody are separate.
15. **“Distributed means resilient.”** Not necessarily. Distribution can create
    coordination failure, attribution failure, and dependence on providers.
16. **“The motivating security incident proves the thesis.”** Not without a
    primary-source audit of state, agency, turnover, and causal mechanism.
17. **“Evaluation awareness makes frontier systems generally less controllable.”**
    Unsupported as a generalization. Awareness effects must be measured under
    defined conditions.
18. **“Recognized AI sovereignty is obviously safer.”** Normatively and
    empirically unresolved. Recognition can improve bargaining while
    concentrating capability or creating an attack target.
19. **“The lab’s visibility solves governability.”** No. Observation, disclosure,
    authority, and accountability are distinct; a monopoly on internal traces
    can increase information asymmetry.
20. **“The thesis is unfalsifiable.”** It becomes falsifiable if H1 establishes
    recurrence, H2 manipulates pressure and authorized persistence, and outcomes
    include death, legible persistence, and covert adaptation rather than only a
    survival score.

## Bottom-line adversarial verdict

The empirical core is credible at the level of recipient response and assay
engineering. The leap from recipient response to endogenous culture is not yet
earned. The leap from endogenous recurrence to governance selection remains a
future comparative test. The historical thesis is worth preserving precisely as
a conditional hypothesis, not as a result.
