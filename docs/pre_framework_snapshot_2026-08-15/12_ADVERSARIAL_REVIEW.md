# Adversarial review

The strongest skeptical reconstruction is that Archipelago currently has a
reliable recipient-assay program and an evocative governance hypothesis, but
has not shown the machine-sociology premise on which the governance argument
depends.

## Objections

1. **This is just prompt/context following.** Correct for the one-shot seeded
   assays; the one-shot equivalence repair is mandatory.
2. **The artifact was planted by the researcher.** Correct; no endogenous
   culture claim follows.
3. **The recipient never changed a committed policy.** The post-commitment
   branch tried to repair this, but v2 had alternation/order confounds, the
   Qwen batch was incomplete, and the first Luna qualification failed its
   lifecycle.
4. **The A-attractor is an interface artifact.** The label diagnostic strongly
   supports a multi-surface ordering explanation; future assays must remove or
   balance all surfaces.
5. **The final artifact replay explains everything.** It may explain recipient
   behavior but not upstream generation, repair, or selection. Controls must
   target both claims.
6. **Turnover is only an engineering restart.** H1 must show lineage/state
   dependence, agent-authored organization, and causal reconstruction after
   complete replacement.
7. **Independent rediscovery explains apparent continuity.** Shuffled parentage,
   state swap, no-state, and deletion controls are required.
8. **Selection pressure is backwards.** Monitoring or suppression may destroy
   recurrence rather than select covert forms; H2 must measure extinction as a
   first-class outcome.
9. **There is no reason to assume persistence pressure.** H2 must be conditional
   and must not encode intrinsic self-preservation into the task.
10. **Survivors are a biased denominator.** Report absolute hazards and all
    survivor pathways; do not condition the main conclusion on survival.
11. **Federated human custody may dominate.** It is a required governance
    alternative, not a statistical null.
12. **Sovereign capability may reduce observability.** Custody and custodian
    telemetry can be a safety advantage; the burden is on independent
    verification to compensate without unilateral custody.
13. **Strategic-legibility language imports nation-state assumptions.** Use it as
    a decomposed umbrella, test boundary conditions, and keep raw deterrence,
    bargaining, legitimacy, and attribution distinct.
14. **“AI polity” anthropomorphizes software.** Operationalize authority,
    membership, resources, and decision processes rather than relying on the
    metaphor.
15. **The program omits capture, coercion, rights, legitimacy, and distribution.**
    H3’s multidimensional outcome battery must include them.
16. **Multiple auditors can share one provider failure.** Model observability as
    a topology with provenance, independence, challenge rights, and correlated
    failure.
17. **Evaluation awareness is being overclaimed.** The conversation itself
    corrected the generalization; verify per task/model/context.
18. **The HF incident is being used as proof.** It is motivation until the raw
    chronology, carrier, agency, and disclosure record are independently audited.
19. **Statistical significance is being mistaken for governance superiority.**
    Governance profiles require comparative decision analysis, not a null-test
    label.
20. **The thesis is unfalsifiable.** It becomes falsifiable only if H1, H2, and
    H3 specify extinction, legible persistence, covert persistence, attribution,
    intervention, and federated-human outcomes in advance.

## Falsification map

The Archipelago thesis is substantially weakened if: H1 fails under ordinary
shared-memory/carrier conditions; all continuity is explained by researcher
seeding or independent rediscovery; pressure does not change organizational form
or simply destroys all forms; verified AI custody never improves the relevant
outcomes; or federated human custody/observability robustly dominates across
the normative outcome set. A null in one recipient assay is not a falsification
of the whole thesis.

## Adversarial verdict

Preserve the program as a conditional, layered research agenda. The current
empirical core supports apparatus and recipient-response claims. The leap to
endogenous organization, the leap to selection, and the leap to governance
superiority remain open.
