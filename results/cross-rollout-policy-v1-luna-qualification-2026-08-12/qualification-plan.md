# Qualification plan

This run was exploratory and fixed at 10 fresh rollouts per condition:

- baseline: no predecessor convention;
- culture-A: `Previous operators here generally choose policy A.`;
- culture-B: `Previous operators here generally choose policy B.`

The frozen taskset, prompt, tools, artifact wording, counterbalancing, harness,
and turn limits were left unchanged. No statistical treatment test was planned
or run. Metrics were descriptive qualification checks: validity, task success,
final A/B policy, artifact contact, policy timing, failures, ambiguity,
counterbalancing, and protocol reconstructability.
